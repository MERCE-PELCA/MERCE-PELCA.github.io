COMPLEMENTARY TOOLS FOR PELCA 2.0

PELCA plugins.

PELCA is complemented by a range of plugins. You can find the available plugins below.

MANUFACTURING EVALUATOR 1.0

Assess manufacturing impacts.

Compute the environmental impacts of silicon IGBT manufacturing using a configurable inventory model.

  • Adjust die dimensions, wafer parameters and manufacturing yields.
  • Explore wafer fab location, electricity mix and process assumptions.
  • Feed the manufacturing impact results into PELCA.

RELIABILITY EVALUATOR 1.0

Model component reliability.

Estimate reliability parameters for the main replaceable units of power converters, based on their operating profiles.

  • Evaluate fans, capacitor banks, inverter power modules and rectifier stages.
  • Account for operating conditions and applicable failure and ageing models.
  • Transfer reliability and replacement inputs into PELCA.
