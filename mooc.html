Introduction to PELCA

PELCA (Power Electronics Life Cycle Assessment) is an open-source project aimed at assessing the environmental impacts over the life cycle of modular and diagnosable power-electronics systems. Modularity and diagnosability support circular-economy practices such as maintenance, repair and reuse. PELCA calculates the impacts associated with the manufacturing, use and replacement of power-electronics products.

Algorithm

A Replaceable Unit (RU) is a part of the system that can be dismantled and replaced during maintenance. When an RU is replaced, its age is reset to zero.

In case (a), six RUs allow individual power switches to be replaced. In case (b), the inverter is a single RU, so the whole system must be replaced when a fault occurs.
